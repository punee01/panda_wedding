KSRTC from Majestic

KSRTC buses leave Kempegowda Bus Station (Majestic) for Shivamogga and Bhadravathi, including Airavat (AC) and Rajahamsa services. The trip takes 5 to 6 hours.

Private AC and sleeper buses

These run mostly overnight. Book on the KSRTC website, redBus or AbhiBus.

From Shivamogga

Local buses to Bhadravathi run every few minutes and take 30 to 40 minutes.

Closest

Fly to Shivamogga (RQY)

Rashtrakavi Kuvempu Airport is about 8 km from Bhadravathi. Direct flights currently come only from Goa, Hyderabad and Tirupati, run by Star Air on select days.

Most flights

Fly to Bengaluru (BLR), then come with us

  • Join our bus from Hebbal at 8:00 am on Thursday 19 Nov, or take a train or KSRTC bus.
  • Reach Bengaluru the night before (18 Nov) or early on the 19th.
  • The airport is 1 to 1.5 hours from the city, so flights landing after about 6 am may miss the bus.
